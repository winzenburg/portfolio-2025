## What the playbook had that the pack lacked (now added)
- A divergent **ideation** capability (new domain ID).
- Named methods throughout (HMW, RICE, Kano, MoSCoW, Crazy 8s, SCAMPER, design sprint, Wizard of Oz, HEART, SUS, fake door, and more).
- Personas, experience maps, and empathy maps (RE-15); competitive and heuristic reviews (RE-13, RE-14); research ethics (RE-18).
- MVP scoping and story mapping (PB-14, PB-15); UX definition of done and staged release (PR-14, PR-15); demand validation and pilots (ME-13, ME-14).
- Design strategy: maturity, roadmap, ROI, DesignOps, working agreements (LG-15–LG-19).
- The phase view itself, plus a worked example in every skill.

## What the pack adds beyond the playbook
- Front-end foundations, architecture, quality, performance, security (FE, AR, QL).
- AI interaction and governance (CX-08–CX-10, CX-13, LG-12–LG-14); growth and conversion surfaces (GM).
- A single accountable lead per skill, depth ratings by role, a scored audit, and **phase exit gates** (the playbook's phases had none).

## Conflicts resolved
- WCAG 2.1 → 2.2 AA (2.2 includes 2.1's criteria plus new ones for focus, targets, and authentication).
- "200–400+ survey responses for significance" → size by margin of error and population.
- Playbook places Design Systems and Interaction Design in Develop only; the pack also applies their QA and debt work in Deliver (see the lens).
